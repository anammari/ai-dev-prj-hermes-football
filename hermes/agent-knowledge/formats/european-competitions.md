# The UEFA Champions League format

The Champions League uses a 36-club league phase, not a traditional group stage. Each club plays eight
different opponents, four at home and four away. Every club is ranked in one table.

At the end of the league phase:

- the top eight clubs go straight to the round of 16;
- clubs placed 9th to 24th enter a two-legged knockout play-off round;
- clubs placed 25th to 36th are eliminated.

The knockout play-offs, round of 16, quarter-finals and semi-finals are two-legged ties decided on
aggregate. The final is a single match. There is no away-goals rule: if the aggregate is level after
the second leg, extra time and then penalties decide the tie.

The competition is not the same as the old 32-club group stage, and the league phase does not use
groups or a fixed bracket before the draw.

## What this note will not say

It does not describe the Europa League or Conference League formats in detail, and it does not
predict results or the draw.
