# How the Championship promotion play-offs work

The top two clubs in the Championship are promoted automatically. The next six clubs — 3rd to 8th —
enter the play-offs, and the winner is promoted as the third club. This six-club format starts in
2026/27; before that, four clubs contested it.

The competition is seven matches:

1. **Quarter-finals, one match each.** 5th plays 8th and 6th plays 7th. The higher-placed club is at home.
2. **Semi-finals, two legs each.** The 3rd-placed club plays the lowest-ranked quarter-final winner, and
   the 4th-placed club plays the highest-ranked quarter-final winner. The higher-placed club has home
   advantage in the second leg.
3. **Final, one match at Wembley.** The two semi-final winners meet; the winner is promoted.

The away-goals rule is not used. If a two-legged semi-final is level on aggregate, the tie goes to
extra time and then penalties under the competition rules.

## What this note will not say

It does not predict the play-offs, name this season's qualifiers, or describe the League One and
League Two formats, which still use four clubs.
