# Clean sheet

A **clean sheet** means a team finishes a match without conceding a goal. It is a defensive statistic,
not a rule in the Laws of the Game: no competition regulation defines the term, and it does not
change a result.

A goalkeeper or defender is credited with a clean sheet when their team concedes no goals in the
match. Different stats providers apply their own minutes-played conventions for individual credit, so
a player who comes on late may or may not be credited. The team-level meaning is the simple one: zero
goals conceded.

A 0-0 draw is a clean sheet for both teams. A 2-0 win is a clean sheet for the winner. A 2-1 win is
not, even if the winning team played well defensively.

## What this note will not say

It does not treat a clean sheet as a competition rule, and it does not invent an official definition
where none exists.
