# Aggregate and the two-legged tie

A two-legged tie is played over two matches, one at each club's ground. The **aggregate** is the sum
of the goals each club scores across both matches. The club with the higher aggregate wins the tie.

Example: Club A wins the first leg 2-1 and loses the second leg 0-1. The aggregate is 2-2. Because
there is no away-goals rule in UEFA club competitions, the tie is level and goes to extra time, then
penalties if still level.

The away-goals rule was abolished in UEFA competitions from 2021/22. A goal scored away from home
counts exactly the same as a goal scored at home. Do not apply the old rule when explaining a modern
tie.

The final of a competition is normally a single match, not a two-legged tie.

## What this note will not say

It does not apply the abolished away-goals rule, and it does not decide a tie that has not been
played.
