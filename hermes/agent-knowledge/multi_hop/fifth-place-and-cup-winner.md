# Fifth place, the FA Cup and a Champions League place

In England, the top four Premier League clubs normally enter the Champions League league phase. The
fifth-placed club enters the Europa League league phase, and the FA Cup winners also enter the Europa
League league phase. The EFL Cup winners enter the Conference League play-off.

If the Premier League earns a fifth Champions League place through the European Performance Spot,
the fifth-placed club goes into the Champions League instead. The Europa League places then move down
the table by one.

Now the multi-hop case: a club finishes fifth **and** wins the FA Cup. Winning the FA Cup does not
create a Champions League place. The club already had a Europa League place from fifth; the FA Cup's
Europa League place passes to the next-highest club that has not qualified for Europe. The club does
not get a Champions League spot from the cup. It would only be in the Champions League if fifth place
itself were a Champions League place that season, or if it qualified some other way.

If a cup winner already qualifies for the Champions League through the league, its cup place passes
down rather than stacking.

## What this note will not say

It does not predict which clubs will finish where, and it does not give another country's cup rules.
