# Serie A tiebreakers

For 2026/27 the FIGC sets different procedures depending on the position and the number of clubs
involved.

**Two clubs level on points for 1st place.** A single play-off is played at the home of the
better-placed club under the *classifica avulsa* criteria. There is no extra time; if the match is
level after 90 minutes, penalties decide it.

**Two clubs level on points for 17th and 18th place.** The relegation play-off is two legs. The
better-placed club hosts the second leg. The club with more goals over the two matches stays up; if
the aggregate is level there is no extra time, and penalties decide it.

**More than two clubs level.** The *classifica avulsa* is compiled first: points in the matches among
the tied clubs, then goal difference in those matches, then overall goal difference, then goals
scored, then a draw. It identifies the two clubs for a play-off, or settles the remaining positions.

**European finalist exception.** If a club that would be in a play-off is a finalist in a UEFA
competition, no play-off is played; the *classifica avulsa* decides the position.

## What this note will not say

It does not predict a play-off, and it does not apply the old head-to-head-first rule from earlier
seasons to 2026/27.
