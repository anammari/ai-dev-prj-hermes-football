# Bundesliga promotion and relegation

The bottom two clubs in the Bundesliga are relegated to Bundesliga 2. The club that finishes 16th
plays the club that finishes 3rd in Bundesliga 2 in a two-legged relegation play-off. The winner
plays in the Bundesliga the following season.

The tie is played under the European Cup system: one match at each club, decided on aggregate. There
is no away-goals rule. If the aggregate is level after the second leg, extra time and then penalties
decide it. The club with fewer match-free days before the first leg hosts the second leg, as set by
the fixture list.

The same play-off structure applies between Bundesliga 2 and the 3. Liga: the 3rd-last club in
Bundesliga 2 plays the 3rd-placed club in the 3. Liga.

## What this note will not say

It does not predict the play-off, and it does not list every promotion rule for the 3. Liga.
